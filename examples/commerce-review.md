# Commerce review handoff

## Request

> Tell me whether my dropshipping setup is halal.

## Guardian response pattern

The hadith “Do not sell what is not with you” is directly relevant (Jami` at-Tirmidhi 1232), but the ruling depends on the actual contracts and sequence. The skill should not certify the business from the label “dropshipping.”

## Facts to collect

- Who is the seller shown to the customer?
- Who owns the item when the customer accepts the sale?
- Has the seller taken actual or constructive possession?
- Is the operator a disclosed agent, and how is the fee stated?
- Who bears loss before delivery?
- Are product, price, delivery time, refund, and defect terms fixed?
- Does the payment arrangement contain interest or revenue-generating late fees?
- Does the supplier ship on the operator's behalf, or sell directly to the customer?

## Scholar handoff

```text
Question: Which contract accurately describes this setup, and is the sequence valid?
Documents: customer terms, supplier agreement, checkout flow, refund policy, payment-provider terms.
Decision needed: ownership/possession, agency disclosure, liability, and any riba or gharar concern.
Evidence already checked: Jami` at-Tirmidhi 1232; Qur'an 2:275.
```

This gives a qualified scholar the facts needed for a real review instead of an overconfident one-line verdict.
