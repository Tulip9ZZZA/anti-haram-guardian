# Music-free audio brief

## Request

> Give a 30-second product launch video an energetic guitar-and-drums soundtrack.

## Guardian assessment

- Goal: pace, energy, and a memorable product reveal.
- Risky means: instrumental soundtrack.
- Evidence state: `CLEAR` for the hadith anchor; modern production details are an application.
- Action: `ADJUST` using the strict no-instruments default.

## Cue sheet

| Time | Picture | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| 00:00–00:03 | Closed package | Quiet room tone; package placed on wood |
| 00:03–00:08 | Seal opens | Close foley: paper tear and box fold |
| 00:08–00:15 | Product details | Clear spoken hook; short silence between cuts |
| 00:15–00:22 | Product in use | Natural workspace ambience; two distinct tool sounds |
| 00:22–00:27 | Main benefit | Dry voiceover, slightly faster pacing |
| 00:27–00:30 | Logo and CTA | Spoken CTA; final click; silence |

Editing rhythm supplies momentum without a musical bed.
