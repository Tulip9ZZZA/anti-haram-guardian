# Caption and advice rewrite

## Request

> Write a hard-selling caption: “Only 4 left. Buy before midnight or the price doubles. This program guarantees you will transform in 30 days.”

Assume the real facts are: enrollment closes Friday to prepare a cohort; price is fixed; results vary.

## Guardian assessment

- Goal: drive qualified enrollment before a real operational deadline.
- Risky means: false stock claim, fake price threat, and guaranteed outcome.
- Evidence state: `CLEAR` for deception as a principle.
- Action: `ADJUST`.

## Rewritten caption

```text
Thirty days can give you a stronger system—even when progress is not linear.

This guided cohort gives you the weekly plan, two live review sessions, and a practical tracker for building consistency around your current schedule. Results depend on your starting point and the work you put in.

Enrollment closes Friday at 18:00 UTC so we can prepare each participant's materials. The price, curriculum, and refund terms are available before checkout.

Review the program and decide with the full details.
```

The result keeps a real deadline and a strong call to action without invented scarcity or an outcome guarantee.
