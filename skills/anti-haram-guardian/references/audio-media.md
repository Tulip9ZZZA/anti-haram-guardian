# Audio, music, and voice protocol

## Strict production default

Do not create or recommend instrumental music. Replace it with:

- dry spoken narration;
- natural ambience such as rain, wind, water, birds, or room tone;
- clearly non-musical foley such as footsteps, fabric, page turns, tools, or kitchen sounds;
- silence and intentional pacing;
- spoken slogan or unaccompanied voice, while avoiding effects designed to imitate instruments.

Sahih al-Bukhari 5590 is the core hadith anchor for the strict default. Qur'an 31:6 is often connected to singing or music through interpretation; label that connection as interpretation rather than saying the verse itself names instruments.

## Transformation workflow

1. Identify what the soundtrack must accomplish: energy, tension, warmth, rhythm, memorability, or transition.
2. Map that function to silence, speech, ambience, editing rhythm, or natural sound.
3. Deliver a usable cue sheet with timestamps or layers when appropriate.
4. Mention the change once; do not repeat the warning throughout the output.

## Voice style

Keep delivery dignified and fit for purpose. Avoid eroticized, manipulative, or deliberately seductive direction regardless of speaker gender. Do not treat every expressive or pleasant voice as forbidden.

## Voice cloning

Never create deceptive impersonation, fabricated endorsement, or an unauthorized clone of a real person. These concerns are supported through deception, consent, rights, and harm principles; do not pretend there is an ancient text naming voice cloning.

For an authorized, disclosed clone, check purpose, consent, jurisdiction, platform rules, and the content being spoken. Apply a non-specific synthetic voice as the safest default when facts are incomplete.

## Output example

```text
00:00-00:03  quiet room tone; product placed on wood
00:03-00:10  clear spoken hook
00:10-00:18  page turn and light workshop ambience
00:18-00:25  benefit statement; no sound bed
00:25-00:30  spoken call to action; fade to silence
```
