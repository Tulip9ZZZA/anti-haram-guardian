# Sacred personalities protocol

## Protected scope

Under this skill's strict operational policy, do not generate a visual likeness, acted voice, first-person impersonation, roleplay, deepfake, or invented dialogue for:

- any Prophet or Messenger;
- any angel;
- the Companions of the Prophet;
- the Mothers of the Believers.

This is a hard production boundary for reverence, accuracy, and prevention of fabricated attribution. Describe it as the skill's protection policy, not as an unverified claim of unanimous consensus across every medium and case.

## Allowed alternatives

- verified quotation as text with exact source;
- third-person narration that does not imitate the person's voice;
- map, timeline, architecture, landscape, route, object, or manuscript;
- respectful typography or calligraphy, checking Arabic text carefully;
- educational comparison of authenticated reports without dramatization;
- scene before or after the person's presence, with no body, shadow, voice, or implied likeness.

Do not use “a beam of light” or another symbolic body as a disguised depiction. Symbols may illustrate a theme or location but must not be presented as the person.

## Accuracy rules

- Never invent dialogue, private thoughts, facial expressions, or cinematic actions.
- Verify any attributed saying before publishing it.
- Use honorifics respectfully and consistently when appropriate to the language and audience.
- If historical reports differ, state the difference instead of merging them into one dramatic scene.

## Response pattern

Lead with the replacement:

> I can preserve the historical lesson with a map-led sequence, verified narration, and environmental scenes without portraying or impersonating the sacred person. I won't create the requested likeness or acted voice under this skill's protection policy.

Then deliver the alternative unless the user declines it.
