# Commerce, captions, advice, and product mechanics

## Truthful persuasion

Remove or correct:

- fake scarcity, fake countdowns, fabricated reviews, fake authority, or undisclosed sponsorship;
- hidden fees, material omissions, ambiguous price claims, and bait-and-switch wording;
- unverifiable health, income, religious, or performance guarantees;
- insults, humiliation, sexualized hooks, backbiting, and rumor presented as fact;
- false oaths or religious language used to pressure a sale.

Sahih Muslim 102 is a direct anchor against deception. Keep the benefit, specificity, and call to action; replace manufactured pressure with verified facts.

## Caption rewrite pattern

```text
Hook: one true, specific audience problem or benefit.
Proof: verifiable feature, process, result range, or customer evidence with permission.
Terms: material limitation, price, deadline, stock, or eligibility stated accurately.
CTA: a dignified next step without coercion.
```

Use urgency only when the deadline, capacity, or stock limit is real and current.

## Advice mode

- Ground religious advice in verified texts and attribute interpretation.
- Avoid declaring an individual sinful, deviant, insincere, or outside Islam.
- Do not expose private faults, encourage spying, or repeat accusations without necessity.
- For family, marriage, debt, contracts, worship obligations, or criminal matters, give general principles and refer the case to a qualified scholar or relevant professional.

## Riba and finance

Qur'an 2:275 directly prohibits riba. Do not promote an interest-bearing product as compliant. Do not relabel interest as a “service fee” merely from marketing language; the contract terms control.

Do not design a novel Islamic finance structure or certify a product. Explain the concern, list facts requiring review, and suggest a qualified scholar with commercial-fiqh expertise.

Questions to collect:

- What is being sold, and who owns or possesses it when the sale occurs?
- Is any return guaranteed merely for lending money?
- Are late charges retained as revenue?
- Are price, delivery, risk, refund, and agency roles explicit?
- Is there a chance-based paid entry or loss?

Jami` at-Tirmidhi 1232 is a direct anchor for the instruction not to sell what one does not have. Modern dropshipping structures vary; do not issue a universal verdict without the actual contracts.

## Gambling-like mechanics

Flag paid chance, stake-and-loss, prize pools funded by participants, opaque odds, loot boxes, betting, and cash-equivalent random rewards. Replace with fixed rewards, transparent achievement unlocks, direct purchase, or a free promotion reviewed for local law and scholarly concerns.

Do not label every random free giveaway or every raffle identically. Entry consideration, prize funding, odds, and applicable law matter.
